The tournament

Mexico's largest lacrosse tournament of the year, with teams from the US, Canada, Colombia, Mexico and more. Every team plays at least five games of two 20-minute halves, with rosters of up to 20 players. Pool play Saturday, playoffs and the championship Sunday.

Stay for the city

Mexico City is one of the great cities of the world: palaces and pyramids, a food scene people fly in for, and neighborhoods that stay awake long after the final whistle.

Saturday evening is free on purpose. Most teams fly home Monday, so there's time to see it properly.

See the night out

After dark

Win or lose, Saturday night in Mexico City is the best part of the trip. The city goes late, and your team will want to keep up.

  • Roma and Condesa Mezcal bars, cocktail spots and rooftops a short walk from each other.
  • Lucha libre Masks, flying wrestlers and a crowd louder than any sideline at Arena México.
  • Plaza Garibaldi Hire a mariachi band, order a round and sing along until late.
  • Tacos at 2 a.m. Al pastor straight off the trompo. The only way to end the night.

By day

Fly in Thursday or leave Monday evening and add one of these.

  • Teotihuacán Climb the Pyramid of the Sun, about an hour outside the city.
  • Xochimilco Rent a trajinera for the team and float the canals with music and drinks.
  • Chapultepec and the Centro A castle in the park, Bellas Artes and the Zócalo, all in one afternoon.

Getting here

  • Fly into MEX Mexico City International (MEX) has direct flights from most major US and Canadian cities. Arrive Friday, leave Monday.
  • Passports and visas US and Canadian passport holders don't need a visa. Other nationalities should check early; we provide official invitation letters on request.
  • Hotel block We're arranging a group rate near the fields. Registered teams get the details first.
  • What to pack Sunscreen, a light jacket for cool mornings, and something nice for the closing party.

Register your team

Register a full team, or sign up on your own as a free agent and we'll place you on a team. Spots are limited by field time and confirmed in the order payments arrive.

  1. Fill in this form. It takes about two minutes.
  2. Pay the team deposit or the free-agent fee to lock your spot.
  3. We send your welcome pack: hotel options, travel guide and roster forms. Free agents get their team assignment before February 1.

No team? No problem. Sign up on your own and we'll place you on a team based on your position and level. You'll meet your teammates at the welcome night.

Questions

Where are the games played?

In Mexico City. The venue will be announced before the early-bird deadline, and registered teams hear first.

Can I come without a team?

Yes. Register as a free agent with your position and level, and we'll build balanced teams from the free-agent list. You can also ask to play with a friend who's already registered.

Can players from different clubs form one team?

Yes. Composite and alumni teams are welcome. One contact registers and manages the roster.

Is there insurance?

Every player signs a waiver. Teams should carry their own liability coverage; contact us if you need help with this.

Is Mexico City safe for visiting teams?

The neighborhoods where teams stay and play are busy with visitors year-round. Our travel guide covers transport, money and areas to enjoy.
